Senior Backend Engineer, Payments
Northwind Pay (fictional), Bengaluru (hybrid)

About the role
You will own services that move money: payment initiation, settlement and reconciliation for UPI, cards and netbanking. You will lead design for new services and mentor two to three engineers.

What you will do
- Design and build high-throughput payment services in Node.js or Go
- Own reconciliation and settlement pipelines end to end
- Lead technical design reviews and mentor engineers on the team
- Improve reliability: observability, alerting, incident response
- Work with compliance on PCI DSS and RBI guidelines

What we are looking for
- 5+ years of backend engineering experience
- Strong Node.js or Go, and PostgreSQL
- Experience with event-driven architecture and message queues (Kafka or RabbitMQ)
- Kubernetes and Terraform in production
- Experience with idempotency, retries and distributed transactions in payment systems
- Track record of mentoring or technical leadership

Nice to have
- gRPC, Prometheus and Grafana
- Experience with PCI DSS compliance
